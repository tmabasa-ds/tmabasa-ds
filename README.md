### 🗺️ Cape Town Housing Crisis - Geospatial Analysis
> *Python · GeoPandas · Folium · Matplotlib · Jupyter*

An analysis of 26,877 Airbnb listings across Cape Town, mapped against official City of Cape Town suburb boundaries to quantify the displacement impact of short-term rentals on local housing.

- Spatial join of listing data to 778 official suburbs, with suburb-level aggregation of pricing, density, and entire-home rates
- Two interactive choropleth maps showing housing pressure and nightly price concentration by suburb
- Affordability gap analysis comparing Airbnb monthly equivalents against Western Cape median salaries
- Full written analysis covering the Woodstock gentrification story, Atlantic Seaboard pricing, and the digital nomad economy

🔗 [View repo](https://github.com/tmabasa-ds/capetown-housing-analysis)

---

### ⚡ Eskom Cost Overruns - Data Analysis
> *Python · Pandas · Matplotlib · Jupyter*

A data analysis of the cost escalation, missed deadlines, and operational failures at Eskom's Medupi and Kusile power stations, using parliamentary SCOPA reports, Eskom annual reports, and SIU findings.

- Tracked cost-to-completion revisions from initial budgets (R150bn combined) to current estimates (R331bn)
- Estimated attribution of the R181bn overrun across six drivers: design defects, inflation, labour disputes, corruption, scope changes, and contractor underperformance
- Charted the paradox of peak capital expenditure coinciding with declining electricity generation
- Timeline visualisation of every missed completion deadline at both stations

🔗 [View repo](https://github.com/tmabasa-ds/eskom-cost-overruns)
